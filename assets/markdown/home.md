Hi. This is Kevin. Pseudo-audiophile. Puzzle lover. California resident my whole life.

I am currently working on a PhD in Electrical Engineering at University of Southern California (started 2022). I've previously obtained a Masters (2021) and a Bachelors (2020) in Computer Science at UCSD. 

At USC, I am currently working with [SAIL](https://sail.usc.edu) (Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory) under Prof. Shrikanth Narayanan. My primary research falls under studying **Speech Production** at the articulator level using real-time MRI with the interdisciplinary [SPAN](https://sail.usc.edu/span/) group, but I also am involved in other projects involving speech analysis and synthesis.

More generally, I have a interest in **Audio Processing** (analysis and synthesis) in the domains of music and speech, studying methods from both traditional signal processing and state of the art machine learning. I also have some interest in Computer Graphics on the side (particularly real-time graphics, animation, and virtual reality).

Asides from programming, I enjoy playing piano and playing games. Although I am classically trained, I more often than not tend to play anison and vocaloid song covers on piano. As for games, I mainly play gacha games, rhythm games, minesweeper, or whatever my friends are playing at the time. I also enjoy a good deal of analog games and puzzles (check out my [WCA](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/persons/2025HUAN16)).
